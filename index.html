How to use this

Read a stage's sections once, at pace. Then work the pushbacks — say your answer out loud before typing anything. The failure mode in these interviews is knowing the material and not being able to say it fluently under pressure, and only the spoken rehearsal fixes that.

Commit even when you're unsure. The gap between what you said and what's written is the actual study material; reading the strong answer first destroys that signal.

The follow-up in each card is the escalation — the second question a good interviewer asks once you've handled the first. Those are where seniority gets assessed, and they're the part to re-run cold in the final week.
